# Getting Started with SwiftBox

Follow these steps to create your account, configure billing, and place your first order.

## 1. Create Your Account

Visit [swiftbox.com/signup](https://swiftbox.com/signup) and complete the registration form. You will need to provide:

- Business name, if applicable
- Contact email
- Phone number
- Billing address

Use an email address that your operations team can access. SwiftBox sends account verification, order confirmations, billing notices, and shipment alerts to this address.

!!! tip
    Use a business email address if you plan to upgrade to Business or Enterprise tiers later.

## 2. Verify Your Email

After submitting the form, check your inbox for a verification email. Click the verification link to activate your account and unlock the SwiftBox dashboard.

If the link expires, return to the sign-in page and request a new verification email.

## 3. Complete Your Profile

Log in to your SwiftBox dashboard and complete your profile settings. This includes:

- Adding saved pickup and delivery addresses
- Setting up payment methods
- Configuring notification preferences
- Choosing default service levels for recurring shipments

Review these settings before creating your first order. Accurate profile details reduce address corrections, payment failures, and notification delays.

## 4. Place Your First Order

Navigate to **New Order** in the dashboard and follow these steps:

1. **Enter recipient details** - Add the recipient name, address, and contact information.
2. **Specify package dimensions** - Enter weight, length, width, and height.
3. **Select a service level** - Choose Standard, Express, or Same-Day delivery.
4. **Choose a pickup option** - Schedule pickup or drop off the shipment at a supported location.
5. **Review and confirm** - Verify all details before submitting the order.

!!! warning
    Double-check the recipient address before confirming. Address corrections after order placement may incur additional fees.

After confirmation, SwiftBox creates the order and assigns a tracking number. Save this tracking number for customer service, dashboard lookup, and API requests.

## 5. Track Your Shipment

Once your order is confirmed, use the tracking number to monitor the shipment in the dashboard or on the [Order Tracking](tracking.md) page. You can also retrieve tracking events through the [Partner API](api.md#get-real-time-tracking-status).

!!! note
    Your first order includes a discount. Check your email for the promo code before you confirm payment.

## Account Deletion and Data Retention

You can request account deletion from **Settings** > **Account** or by emailing [support@swiftbox.com](mailto:support@swiftbox.com). SwiftBox retains order, billing, and tax records as required for legal, security, and compliance purposes. Personal data that is no longer required is deleted or anonymized according to the SwiftBox retention policy.

## Troubleshooting

### Did not receive the verification email?

Check your spam or quarantine folder, confirm that the email address is correct, and request a resend from the sign-in page. If the message still does not arrive, contact [support@swiftbox.com](mailto:support@swiftbox.com).

### Cannot add a payment method?

Confirm that your billing address matches the payment method on file. For supported payment options by plan, see [Pricing & Billing](billing.md#payment-methods).

### Tracking number not found?

Tracking numbers may take a few minutes to appear after order confirmation. If the status is still unavailable after 15 minutes, verify the order ID in your dashboard or contact support.
