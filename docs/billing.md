## Billing Cycle

Billing is processed monthly on the anniversary of your account activation. Plan charges, surcharges, taxes, and usage-based fees appear on the same invoice.

Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle unless otherwise stated during checkout.

## Payment Methods

SwiftBox supports the following payment methods:

- **Credit and debit cards** - Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
- **ACH bank transfers** - Available for Business and Enterprise accounts in supported regions.
- **Net-30 invoices** - Available to approved Enterprise customers.

Keep at least one active payment method on file to avoid service interruption.

## Taxes

Listed prices do not include taxes. Applicable taxes are calculated from the billing address and shown before payment is confirmed.

- **United States** - Sales tax may apply based on state and local rules.
- **European Union** - VAT may apply unless a valid VAT ID is provided and accepted.

Tax rates and exemptions can change. Review each invoice for the final tax amount charged.

## Billing History

You can download invoices and receipts from the dashboard:

1. Go to **Billing** > **Invoices**.
2. Select the billing period.
3. Choose **Download PDF** or **Download CSV**.

Invoices include the plan, shipment usage, surcharges, taxes, payment method, and billing address used for that period.

## Refund Policy

Monthly plans do not include refunds for unused shipments or unused time in the current billing period. Annual plans may be eligible for prorated refunds when required by contract or local law.

Shipment fees are refundable only when SwiftBox cancels the shipment before pickup or when support confirms a billing error.

## FAQ

### How do I upgrade my plan?

Navigate to **Settings** > **Plan** in your dashboard and select your desired tier. Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle unless the checkout flow states otherwise.

!!! note
    Upgrading mid-cycle prorates the additional charges. Downgrading takes effect at the next cycle with no refunds for unused shipments.

### What payment methods are accepted?

Starter accounts can pay by credit or debit card. Business accounts can use cards or ACH bank transfers. Enterprise customers can request ACH payments and net-30 invoice terms.

### Are there additional fees?

- **Address corrections**: $5 per correction
- **Saturday delivery**: $15 surcharge
- **Signature required**: $3 per shipment
- **Insurance above $100**: $0.50 per $100 of coverage

!!! warning
    Failed payments incur a $25 processing fee. SwiftBox provides a 7-day grace period to update your payment method before account suspension.

### Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel at any time from your dashboard. Service continues through the end of your current billing period with no prorated refunds for monthly plans.

!!! tip
    Contact our sales team for volume discounts if your monthly shipping volume exceeds Enterprise tier limits.
